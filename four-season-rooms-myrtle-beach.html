Why a four-season room

More months, more weather

A screened porch is great in good weather. A four-season room closes it in with windows so you can use it on cooler, windy or rainy days too.

Light and view

Windows let the light and the view in, while keeping the rain, pollen and bugs out.

Four-season rooms for Grand Strand homes

Many Grand Strand homes already have a covered porch or a screened porch. Often that space can be closed in with windows to make a room you use all year.

We match the framing color and roofline so the room looks original to the home, not added on. If you want heating and cooling in the room, we will talk through that at the estimate.

Enclosed rooms need a permit more often than a screen-in. We will tell you what your town requires and give you a clear written price before any work starts.
