How Much Does a Screened Porch Cost in Myrtle Beach?

By Tom Shipley, owner of Simply Enclosures · Myrtle Beach, SC · November 24, 2026

"What's this going to cost me?" is the first thing almost everybody wants to know, and most companies won't say until they're sitting at your kitchen table. I'd rather tell you up front. Here's how we price screening in a covered porch, with real math.

About $125 per foot of screen wall
+ $530 per screen door

Installed. White aluminum framing, standard screen and a chair rail. That's for screening in a porch that already has a roof over it.

How to figure your own ballpark

  1. Measure the open sides of your porch, the sides that will get screen. Don't count the wall that's against the house.
  2. Add those feet together and multiply by $125.
  3. Add $530 for each screen door you want.

What moves the price

  • How many feet of open wall you have. That's the biggest one.
  • How many doors. Each screen door is $530 installed.
  • No roof yet? Screening needs a covered porch to attach to. If you only have an open patio or slab, that's a bigger project and we'll talk it through at the estimate.

Want to use it in winter, too?

Turning a screened porch into a four-season room with windows is a different job. A recent screen-room-to-windows conversion we priced came in around $11,700, and most land roughly $9,500 to $14,500 depending on the windows and doors. Window prices come from our supplier, so we give you an exact number after we measure. More on that: sunrooms and four-season rooms.

Already have a screened porch?

If the screen is torn or sagging, you probably don't need a new porch. We do screen repair and re-screening too.
