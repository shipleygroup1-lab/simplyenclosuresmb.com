Porches for Conway homes

Conway is Horry County's county seat, with its historic downtown along the Waccamaw River, older homes with big front porches, and newer neighborhoods spreading out from Highway 501 and Highway 544. Larger lots and lots of trees mean lots of shade, and lots of mosquitoes.

Whether you have a classic front porch or a covered back patio, screening it in lets you sit outside in the evening without the bugs. We use powder-coated aluminum framing that will not rot like wood.

Some Conway neighborhoods have HOAs and some do not. Either way, we give you a drawing and a clear written price before any work starts.

What Conway homeowners usually ask for

Front porch screen-ins

Older homes with deep front porches are a great fit for screen. We keep the look of the house while keeping the bugs out.

Back porch and patio

Turn a covered back porch or patio into a screened room for grilling and family time.

Screen repair

Already have a screened porch with torn panels? We re-screen and repair. Screen repair has a $150 minimum service call.

Options we build with

Standard or no-see-um screen

Our standard is charcoal fiberglass screen, which is easy to see through. If you get no-see-ums at dusk, ask about finer 20x20 no-see-um screen.

Pet screen and kick plates

Dogs and kids are hard on the bottom of a screen. A chair rail with tougher pet screen or a solid kick plate below it keeps the lower panels in one piece.

Framing color and doors

Powder-coated aluminum framing in colors that match your trim, with one or more screen doors placed where you actually walk.
