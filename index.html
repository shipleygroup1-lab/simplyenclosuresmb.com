FAQ

Common questions.

Is the estimate really free?

Yes. We come out, measure the space, talk through your options and give you a clear price. No cost and no obligation.

Can you screen in a porch I already have?

Yes. Most of our work is enclosing existing covered porches and patios. We fit the framing to your openings so it looks built-in.

What colors does the framing come in?

White and bronze aluminum. We'll help you pick the one that best matches your trim, windows and roofline.

Are you licensed?

Yes. Simply Enclosures work is performed under South Carolina Specialty Contractor License #63242.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the size and layout of the porch. You'll get a timeline along with your estimate so you know what to expect.

What areas do you serve?

Myrtle Beach and the Grand Strand, including North Myrtle Beach, Surfside Beach, Garden City, Murrells Inlet, Pawleys Island and Conway.

Can I see what my porch could look like first?

Yes. Use our porch builder to pick a size, frame color, door and screen, and see it in 3D. Send it to us and we'll bring it to your free measure.
