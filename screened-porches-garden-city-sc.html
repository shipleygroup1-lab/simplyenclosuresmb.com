Porches in Garden City

Beach and marsh homes

Garden City sits between Surfside Beach and Murrells Inlet, with the ocean on one side and marsh on the other. The marsh side especially brings bugs at dusk.

Built for salt air

Near the beach, wood framing and cheap hardware do not last. We use powder-coated aluminum framing made for the coast.

Porches for Garden City homes

Garden City Beach is a mix of full-time homes, second homes and rentals between Surfside Beach and Murrells Inlet. Covered porches and decks are everywhere, and screening them makes them usable in the evenings when the no-see-ums come out.

For homes close to the marsh, ask about finer 20x20 no-see-um screen. It keeps out the tiny biting bugs that get through standard screen.

Many beach homes are raised, with porches up off the ground. We look at the porch at the estimate and give you a clear written price before any work starts.

Options we build with

Standard or no-see-um screen

Our standard is charcoal fiberglass screen, which is easy to see through. If you get no-see-ums at dusk, ask about finer 20x20 no-see-um screen.

Pet screen and kick plates

Dogs and kids are hard on the bottom of a screen. A chair rail with tougher pet screen or a solid kick plate below it keeps the lower panels in one piece.

Framing color and doors

Powder-coated aluminum framing in colors that match your trim, with one or more screen doors placed where you actually walk.
